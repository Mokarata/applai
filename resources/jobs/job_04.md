* **Industry:** Automotive, Industrial Technology, Consumer Goods, Energy and Building Technology.

**Qualifications:**

* Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field with a focus on AI/ML.
* Strong programming skills in C++ and/or Python.
* Experience with machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch).
* Knowledge of embedded systems and real-time operating systems is a plus.
