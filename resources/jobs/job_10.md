
# Software Engineer, AI Applications

**Location:** Munich, Germany

**Company Information:**

* **Industry:** Technology (Graphics Processing Units (GPUs), AI hardware and software).

NVIDIA is seeking a Software Engineer, AI Applications, to join their team in Munich. You will be working on cutting-edge AI applications leveraging NVIDIA's hardware and software platforms.

* Collaborate with researchers and other engineers to push the boundaries of AI.

**Qualifications:**

* Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
* Strong programming skills in C++ and/or Python.
* Experience with deep learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch).
* Familiarity with GPU computing and parallel programming.
